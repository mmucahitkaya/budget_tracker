## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Receipts and card statements read by a local model (Ollama); rule-based parsing of text PDFs
- Credit cards, statements, partial payments, installments shown month by month
- Recurring income and expenses (last business day, varying amounts, one-off payments, raise reminders)
- Spendable this month / next month, 14-month plan, savings insights, budgets and reports
- Fast categorization with merchant learning, notes and marketplace orders
- Investments (stocks, funds, metals, currencies) and savings goals
- Optional Telegram bot
- First-run setup: region pack, number/date format, base currency and extra currencies
